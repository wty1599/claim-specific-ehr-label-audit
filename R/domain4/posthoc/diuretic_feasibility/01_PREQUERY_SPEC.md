# Post hoc D4 loop-diuretic feasibility specification

Written 2026-09-25 08:22:58 +08:00, before any cohort-specific diuretic timing, treatment-support, mortality-cell, or threshold result was generated. This analysis was proposed after the original RRT D4 result was known. It is not part of the original prespecified D4 analysis. The author selected loop diuretics as the only additional treatment, selected nested risk-set option A, and prohibited trying a second drug if this one fails.

## Question and protected objects

Question: among patients in the original 17,525-person ICU-hour-24 RRT-free risk set who had no recorded ICU IV loop-diuretic administration before hour 24, is there enough observed support in both fixed K2 groups to develop a comparison of first loop-diuretic administration during ICU hours [24,72)? The question is support/feasibility, not treatment effect or heterogeneity. Do not modify the original D4 patient ledger, K2 labels, RRT outputs, cohort definition, first-event rules, 30-day mortality anchor, or D1-D3 analyses. All patient-level data remain private and local.

The original 17,525 patients are the **anchor pool**, not automatically the diuretic denominator. Option A removes pre-hour-24 IV loop users from that pool; both the anchor and smaller eligible denominator must be reported by K2 group. Pre-ICU or home oral/IV medication use cannot be reliably identified from this source and is not an exclusion. This is an ICU-recorded new-use proxy, not a verified pharmacologic new-user design.

## Drug and administration definition, fixed before querying cohort results

The read-only local `mimiciv_icu.d_items` lookup identified three `inputevents` medication items: 221794 Furosemide (Lasix), 228340 Furosemide (Lasix) 250/50, and 229639 Bumetanide (Bumex). All three have `d_items.category = Medications`, `linksto = inputevents`, and `unitname = mg`; **none has a route-specific dictionary category**. No torsemide/torasemide entry matched in `d_items`; it is not inferred from another source. Treat both furosemide items as the same drug class. Count a row from `mimiciv_icu.inputevents` only when its itemid is in this fixed set, `amount > 0`, its order category is `01-Drips` or `05-Med Bolus`, its status is one of `Bolus`, `ChangeDose/Rate`, `FinishedRunning`, `Paused`, or `Stopped`, its start/end times are nonmissing and ordered, and its interval overlaps the index ICU stay (`endtime > intime`, `starttime < outtime`). The source-level inspection before cohort querying found only these categories/statuses for the three items, with the medication amounts normally in mg and two furosemide bolus rows in `dose` units. Do not impose a dose threshold, duration threshold, or mg conversion. Oral drugs and eMAR records do not enter the treatment definition; eMAR may be summarized descriptively later but cannot rescue a failed result.

`inputevents` is an ICU administration-record source but does not carry a per-row route field, and the observed `Medications` dictionary category does not independently prove IV route. The analysis must therefore be labelled an **inputevents-recorded IV-administration proxy**, not verified IV dosing. A positive-amount row represents a recorded delivery segment, not proof of a clinically meaningful de-resuscitation strategy. Use `starttime` as the segment/start time, not `storetime` or an order timestamp. Multiple dose/rate-change rows under the same order are not counted as multiple starts; retain the earliest qualifying start per stay. No information from after hour 24 is used to define the no-prior-IV-loop exclusion.

For prior use, exclude any qualifying dose whose interval begins before ICU hour 24 and overlaps `[ICU intime, ICU hour 24)`, including a segment begun just before ICU arrival that continues into ICU. A segment starting exactly at hour 24 is eligible as a window administration. For the primary window, candidate first dose starts at or after ICU hour 24 and strictly before hour 72. Doses after ICU exit are invalid. A segment starting before hour 24 and running across the landmark excludes the patient rather than becoming a new start at hour 24.

## Event ledger and timing stop

Retain the original RRT risk-set filter: alive, in the index ICU and without recorded RRT before hour 24. For eligible patients, record the first event in `[24,72)` among qualifying IV loop start, original RRT first-record time, death, ICU exit, and hour-72 administrative end. For exact ties within the original 1e-8-hour tolerance, use **RRT > IV loop > death > ICU exit > administrative end**. The RRT-first preference prevents concurrent renal replacement from being silently counted as an isolated loop-diuretic opportunity; it is a new treatment-specific rule, not a retroactive change to the original RRT ledger. Report tie counts and the first-event distribution. A loop start after earlier RRT, death or ICU exit is not treated. The binary model, if reached, describes a first recorded loop administration before those competing/precluding events, not assignment at hour 24.

First perform only source extraction and timing assessment. Among anchor-pool patients with a first qualifying administration segment overlapping the ICU stay and starting before hour 72 **and before the earliest original RRT record, death, or ICU exit**, count first starts before hour 24 (including segments starting before ICU entry but continuing into the stay) versus `[24,72)`. Use the same tie priority below: RRT wins an exact tie, whereas loop administration wins an exact tie with death or ICU exit. Report pre-ICU-overlap and post-RRT-first loop records separately rather than silently assigning them to late starts. If more than half of these opportunity-valid first loop records start before hour 24, the proposed hour-24 initiation question fails its stated clinical-timing condition: stop after the timing/denominator report and do not fit support models or try another treatment. Also stop for an empty or unjoinable source, mismatched original anchor counts, or unresolvable administration semantics.

## Feasibility measures if the timing condition holds

Within C1/C2, report eligible n; loop-first n/rate; 30-day death, survival and missing-outcome counts separately in loop-first and non-loop-first cells; and initiation-time distribution. Thirty-day mortality retains its original admission anchor. Refit the **same original D4 covariate formula** with loop-first as the binary record outcome: K2, age, gender, SOFA, first-day AKI stage, SAPS II, maximum creatinine/BUN/potassium/lactate/heart rate/respiratory rate, minimum bicarbonate/pH/mean BP/GCS and first-day urine total. Reuse the original median/mode missing-value handling, K2/gender factors, unpenalized binomial GLM, logit link, and probability clip `[1e-6, 1-1e-6]`. Record warnings, convergence, missingness, model n and clipping; do not tune covariates for a better verdict. Previously identified hour-24 SOFA and SAPS II timing caveats carry forward and limit causal interpretation.

Compute the original un-stabilized ATE weights (`1/e` for loop-first, `1/(1-e)` otherwise), original pooled ESS/N, and original empirical min/max propensity-range intersection within each K2 group. Report outside-overlap percentages overall and by record status, and weights >10 as a percentage of **all patients in that K2 group**. Also report treatment-arm and non-treatment-arm ESS and their absolute cell sizes so a pooled ratio does not obscure scarcity. Original support criteria are unchanged: in both K2 groups, loop-first frequency >=1% and at least 10 observed admission-anchored 30-day deaths in each loop-first and non-loop-first cell; structural alerts are outside overlap >10%, pooled ESS/N <0.25, minimum treatment-status cell <30, or >1% of all group weights >10. All conditions are evaluated at full precision. If either group fails a criterion or triggers a structural alert, the additional treatment is support-inadequate under the **original rule**. Even a clean support screen authorizes only later model development, not an effect estimate. No actual RRT or loop-diuretic effect model may be fitted here.

For descriptive context, juxtapose the new loop result with the archived RRT anchor result, clearly labeling the different eligibility denominator and new RRT-competing event rule. Do not recompute or alter original RRT weights to make the two treatments appear directly equivalent.

## Single prespecified sensitivity and explanatory check

Exclude patients with `mimiciv_derived.charlson.congestive_heart_failure = 1`, joined by `subject_id` and `hadm_id`, from the nested eligible group and report whether the original-rule support conclusion changes. The field is derived from hospitalization diagnosis codes, not home medication history, and can reflect information entered after hour 24; this is a descriptive coded-CHF exclusion, not a prospective baseline restriction. A missing Charlson row is reported as unknown, not silently coded absent. No other diagnosis definition or subset will be tried. The primary analysis is unchanged.

If the main support model is estimable, an optional non-decisive explanatory check fits two *separate* fixed seven-variable logistic models on the same nested eligible patients: one for loop-first and one for the original first-RRT record. Predictors are first-day AKI stage, maximum creatinine/BUN/potassium, minimum bicarbonate/pH, and first-day urine total. Report apparent ROC AUCs and event counts only; the model outcomes have different competing-event definitions, and AUCs are descriptive associations rather than proof that one treatment has an independent indication. No alternate predictor block, treatment, risk set or model will be selected from the results.
